Cairo Coding keeps everything about your Cairo Coding School course in one app, so you always know when your next session is and how to join it.

⏱ YOUR NEXT SESSION, FRONT AND CENTER
Open the app and see your next session: the day, the time, and a live countdown. When it's time, tap "Join meeting" and you're in class.

📅 THE FULL SCHEDULE
Every upcoming session for your group, plus the ones you already attended. If a session is moved, cancelled, or an extra one is added, it's clearly marked, so you never show up at the wrong time.

🎥 WATCH IT AGAIN
Missed a session or want to review pointers one more time? Every recording is listed with its session number and date. In more than one group? Filter by group. Recordings open in YouTube or Google Drive.

🌍 ARABIC OR ENGLISH, LIGHT OR DARK
Use the app in Arabic or English, with full right-to-left support. Pick light, dark, or follow your phone's theme.

🔐 MADE FOR ENROLLED STUDENTS
There's no sign-up. Cairo Coding School creates your account when you enroll, and you set your own password from the email you receive. You only ever see your own groups.

🙌 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads. No tracking. Your schedule and recordings are only visible to the students of your group.
